## ✅ **Key Tips for Simple Prompts**

1. **Use keywords**: Include the main concept (e.g., "outbreak", "correlation", "predict")
2. **Keep it short**: 3-7 words usually work best
3. **Be specific**: Mention what you want to analyze (e.g., "blood sugar", "age", "disease")
4. **Use natural language**: Write as you would speak

---

## 🚫 **What to Avoid**

- ❌ Too complex: "Analyze disease patterns and outbreak trends across different regions"
- ✅ Simple: "Disease outbreak across regions"

- ❌ Too vague: "Analyze data"
- ✅ Specific: "Average blood sugar"

- ❌ Multiple concepts: "Analyze and compare and predict"
- ✅ One concept: "Predict diabetes"

---

## 🎯 **Quick Reference**

| Want to... | Use Prompt Like... |
|------------|-------------------|
| Find average | "Average [metric]" |
| Find relationship | "How are [X] and [Y] related?" |
| Predict something | "Predict [outcome] using [features]" |
| Find patients | "List patients with [condition]" |
| Compare groups | "Compare [group1] and [group2]" |
| Detect problems | "Find abnormal [metric]" |
| Analyze disease spread | "Disease outbreak" or "Disease spreading" |
| Train ML model | "Train model to predict [outcome]" |

---

## 💡 **Examples That Work**

✅ **Good Examples:**
- "Average blood sugar"
- "Correlation between age and blood sugar"
- "Predict diabetes"
- "Disease outbreak"
- "Find abnormal patients"
- "Compare patient groups"

❌ **Avoid:**
- "I would like to analyze the comprehensive dataset to understand the intricate patterns..."
- "Please perform a detailed statistical analysis..."
- "Can you help me with data analysis?"

---

**Remember**: The system is smart enough to understand simple, natural language. Just say what you want in plain English!
